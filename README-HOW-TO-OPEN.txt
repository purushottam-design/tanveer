TANVEER SHAIKH - SAME-UI STATIC BOOTSTRAP EDITION

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the entire ZIP. Do not move individual files out of their folders.
2. Double-click index.html in Chrome / Microsoft Edge. No npm, Python, or server required.
3. For hosting: upload the ENTIRE folder contents to public_html or any static hosting service.

- Original compiled GSAP, Lenis and Three.js animations: assets/portfolio.js
