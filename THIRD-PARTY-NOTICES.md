## Photographs and video

- Two comedy videos and the group photograph were retrieved from the public
  @tanveer_shaikh_official profile archive indexed at
  https://imginn.com/tanveer_shaikh_official/.
  The archive exposed public Instagram CDN files. Creator ownership is retained.
- The profile picture was retrieved through the Modash public creator listing:
  https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/india/amravati.
- The film artwork and premiere photograph were published in Mahavoice:
  https://mahavoice.com/amravati-ke-potte-a-story-make-you-laugh-and-cry/.
  The film's artwork is credited in its description to Vaibhav Dhole /
  U Motion Picture. The original creators retain ownership.
- Reel posters are frame extractions from the included videos, not independent
  portraits or claims of separate photoshoots.
- Amravati Ke Potte is embedded from its publisher, Aarish Shah19:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=biI3D6gS7Aw.
  The full film is not included as a local download.
- Instagram retains control over the linked post embeds.

Media was resized or recompressed for website performance. No new face or
fabricated photo/video of the subject was generated.
